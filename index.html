Sustainability Initiatives

Beauty you can feel good about — from the brands we choose to the box that arrives at your door.

As a curated beauty retailer, we don't manufacture our own products — but that gives us a unique responsibility: choosing partners, packaging, and practices that reflect our values. Here's how sustainability shows up across everything we do.

Responsible Sourcing

We prioritize stocking brands that are cruelty-free, vegan, and transparent about their ingredients and supply chains. Products meeting these criteria are clearly marked across our shop.

Sustainable Packaging & Shipping

Every order ships in right-sized, recyclable packaging — no oversized boxes, no unnecessary plastic. Customers can also choose carbon-neutral shipping at checkout.

Empty Container Take-Back

Send back your empty containers and get a discount on your next order. We partner with a specialized recycler to responsibly process hard-to-recycle beauty packaging.

Customer Education

Our blog regularly features guides on sustainable beauty routines, how to recycle empties properly, and spotlights on the eco-conscious brands we carry.

Community Partnership

We donate a portion of profits to local environmental organizations, supporting causes that align with our commitment to a healthier planet.
